01Statistical learning theory

An Asymptotic One-Sample Surrogate for Non-Gaussian Empirical Risk Minimization (ERM) with General Convex Regularization

A one-sample surrogate that keeps the original penalty and the feature–label law to study prediction beyond Gaussian assumptions.

Non-Gaussian ERMConvex regularization
Read paper ↗
Methods & contributions
  • Studied high-dimensional ERM with correlated, non-Gaussian features and convex regularization (e.g., ℓ1 regularization with pseudo-Huber loss)
  • Constructed a one-sample surrogate with asymptotic mean and prediction-variance transfer; derived asymptotic prediction laws and expressions for classification error
  • Combined cavity Hamilton–Jacobi interpolation, leave-one-out analysis, and convex response calculus to handle nonsmooth penalties
  • Retained feature–label distributional information in the surrogate, explaining the breakdown of Gaussian universality in prediction and variable selection

The paper’s prediction-transfer results are conditional on its fresh-score conjecture and stated assumptions. Transfer of support statistics remains open.

02Sampling & optimization

Tempered Proximal Sampling Algorithms

Combine an implicit drift step with state-scaled Gaussian noise to explore heavy-tailed distributions.

Langevin dynamicsHeavy tails
Methods & contributions
  • Developed a tempered proximal Langevin sampler combining implicit drift updates with state-dependent Gaussian noise to stabilize superlinear dynamics and improve heavy-tail exploration
  • Established geometric ergodicity under tail-growth conditions, covering polynomial-tailed targets for which non-tempered proximal sampling fails to be geometrically ergodic
  • Formulated tempered Langevin dynamics as a gradient flow in weighted Wasserstein geometry, providing a variational framework for convergence and discretization-error analysis

03Machine learning · Computer vision

Human Motion Similarity Evaluation Framework Using Deep Learning

Compare movements across videos by reconstructing joint trajectories and aligning their timing.

MotionBERTDynamic time warping
Methods & contributions
  • Designed a framework to evaluate motion similarity from 2D videos under varying conditions (e.g., angles, lighting)
  • Used AlphaPose to extract 2D joint-coordinate sequences and fine-tuned MotionBERT to reconstruct 3D joint trajectories; aligned motion sequences using dynamic time warping (DTW) with cosine distance
  • Achieved 93% accuracy in recognizing and matching key actions across videos

04Algorithms · Parallel computing

N-Queens Solver with Bitwise and Parallel Computing

Encode board constraints as bit masks, prune conflicts, and split the search across independent branches.

Bit masksOracle SQL
Methods & contributions
  • Implemented a single Oracle SQL query to solve the N-Queens problem using backtracking and bitwise operations
  • Encoded queen placements as bit masks, reducing storage requirements by over 80% and achieving a computational speedup of over 3× relative to numeric and string-based representations
  • Partitioned the search space for parallel computation and tuned Oracle memory allocation; solved the 18-Queens problem on a home computer within one week, setting a university record
